## This website

This marketing site is static. It sets **no cookies**, runs **no analytics** and loads nothing from third parties.
Links into the app carry an `entry` parameter (for example `?entry=home-hero`) so we can tell which page a visit
came from; it identifies the link, not you.

## The app

When you sign in to the app at `app.codebench.localhost`:

| What | Why | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email address and account id | To sign you in and recognise your account | Our authentication provider (Clerk) and our database |
| Role and plan | To decide what you can open | Our database |
| Code you run or submit, and its results | To grade it and show you your history | Our database |
| Editor drafts and your theme choice | So you don't lose work | Your browser only (`localStorage`) |

Signing in uses session cookies set by the authentication provider. We don't sell or share your data, and we
don't use it for advertising.

## Deleting your data

Delete your account from the provider's account screen. We then remove your email address, disable the account and
keep past submissions only in anonymised form, so aggregate statistics stay correct.

This page will be updated before any paid plan or payment provider is added.
